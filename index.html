        This static HTML simulator has no backend and no external dependency. The 48h step is modelled as an internal readiness checkpoint to prepare the 72h notification, not as a separate formal CRA deadline unless your internal process defines it that way. Version 5.0 includes guided Next navigation, field-level notification type selection, a final summary screen, a visible full reset action, and hardened scroll stability while typing.
